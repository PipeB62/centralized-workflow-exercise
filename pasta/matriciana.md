🍝 Spaghetti alla Matriciana
A classic Roman pasta with guanciale, tomato, and Pecorino Romano

## Ingredients
(2 servings)
200 g spaghetti

100 g guanciale, cut into strips

200 g canned peeled tomatoes (or passata)

40 g Pecorino Romano, grated

1 small chili pepper (optional)

Salt (only for the pasta water)

Black pepper, to taste

## Instructions
1. Render the guanciale
Place the guanciale in a cold pan and heat gently.

Let it cook until the fat melts and the meat becomes crispy.

Remove the guanciale and keep it aside; leave the fat in the pan.

2. Prepare the sauce
Add the tomatoes to the pan with the guanciale fat.

Crush them lightly and simmer for 10–15 minutes.

Add chili pepper if you like a bit of heat.

Return the guanciale to the sauce at the end.

3. Cook the pasta
Boil spaghetti in salted water until al dente.

4. Combine
Transfer the spaghetti directly into the sauce.

Toss vigorously over medium heat.

Add grated Pecorino Romano and a splash of pasta water to create a creamy emulsion.

5. Serve
Plate immediately.

Finish with extra Pecorino and freshly ground black pepper.
