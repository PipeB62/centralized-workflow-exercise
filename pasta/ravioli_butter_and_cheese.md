Ravioli with Butter and Cheese
A simple, classic Northern Italian preparation

## Ingredients 
(2 servings)

250 g fresh ravioli (your preferred filling)

60 g unsalted butter

40–50 g grated Parmigiano Reggiano or Grana Padano

A few fresh sage leaves (optional but traditional)

Salt, to taste

Freshly ground black pepper (optional)

## Instructions
Boil the ravioli  
Bring a large pot of salted water to a gentle boil. Add the ravioli and cook until they float and are tender (usually 2–4 minutes, depending on thickness).

Prepare the butter sauce  
In a small pan, melt the butter over medium heat.

If using sage, add the leaves and let them sizzle until fragrant.

You can let the butter foam and turn lightly golden for a nuttier flavor, but avoid burning it.

Combine  
Using a slotted spoon, transfer the cooked ravioli directly into the butter pan. Toss gently to coat without breaking them.

Finish with cheese  
Remove from heat. Add the grated cheese and toss again so it melts into a creamy coating.
If needed, add a spoonful of pasta cooking water to emulsify.

Serve  
Plate immediately and top with extra cheese and a twist of black pepper.
